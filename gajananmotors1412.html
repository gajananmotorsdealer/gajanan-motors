Honesty, inspection, and customer-first service — that is our promise.

Who we are

Gajanan Motors is a family-run pre-owned car dealer established to make buying and selling used cars simpler and more reliable. We focus on accurate condition reporting, fair pricing and friendly service to help customers make confident decisions.

How we work

  1. Initial appraisal and photo review
  2. Multi-point inspection by our technicians
  3. Clear condition report and honest valuation
  4. Facilitate test-drive and paperwork assistance
  5. After-sale support and guidance

Our values

Integrity, transparency and respect for customers' time and investment. We disclose inspection results and provide straight answers.
